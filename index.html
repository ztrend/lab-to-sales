"Scientist by training, salesperson by necessity."

A 5-pillar coaching program teaching trained scientists and technical founders a complete sales framework and behavioral manual.

The five pillars
01

Industry & Sales Environment

Industry diagnosis. We take a zoomed out perspective of your industry, the position of your company in it, the technology wave you are in and the complexity you are operating to evaluate the direction of your sales.

  • Product Complexity Tiers
  • Technology Waves & Cycle Positioning
  • Market Context & Regulation
  • Competitive Landscape
  • Company Context
02

Sales Strategy

Knowing your sales targets we build your yearly sales strategy. We use the 80/20 rule and find the most important relationships and map your ideal client profile to focus your limited time on.

  • Yearly Target & Revenue Mapping
  • Relationship Prioritization
  • Multiyear Positioning
  • Ideal Client Profile & Buyer Spectrum
03

Sales Cycle

We analyse your day-to-day workflow — from first contact to closed deal to long-term account management. We use the sales cycle and your sales targets to evaluate the best possible actions. We go deeper into the stages you want to focus on.

  • Prospecting & Lead Generation
  • Discovery & Qualification
  • Consultative Conversation
  • Objection Handling
  • Closing
  • Post-Sales & Account Management
04

Core Competencies

The behavioral level for converting conversations into sales. Here we focus on emotional awareness, building rapport and trust, while sustaining your authentic voice for communication.

  • Presence & Emotional Awareness
  • Active Listening & Questioning
  • Trust Building
  • Authentic Communication
  • Stakeholder Navigation
05

Systems & Operations

We build your CRM operating system, so that your energy focuses on strategy and human interaction. We automate your operations, use AI and other automating tools for your specific use case.

  • CRM Architecture
  • Pipeline Management
  • Operational Rhythm
  • Follow-up & Cadence Systems
